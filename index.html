01 — The studio

The line between a film and a game is a choice.

Karayel Games is an independent interactive film studio. We write branching scripts, shoot them on real locations with real performers, and turn every scene into a decision the player owns.

Our name comes from the karayel — the north-west wind of Istanbul. It changes direction without warning. So do our stories.

03 — Reality Lab · R&D

Today you choose what the camera sees. Next, you direct who stands in front of it.

Reality Lab is our research track. The goal: a live direction layer where a player's decision reaches a real performer, in a real place, in real time — an earpiece cue, a mark on the ground, a new line to say. The scene you see is not pre-recorded. It is happening because you chose it.

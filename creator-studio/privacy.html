Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers Yayin Studyo, an internal publishing tool operated by Karayel Games ("we"). Yayin Studyo is not a public application. It is used only by our own team to upload our own original short videos to our own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook channels.

YouTube API Services

Yayin Studyo uses YouTube API Services. By using it, our channel owners agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • OAuth authorization for our own YouTube channels (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).
  • Basic statistics of our own channels and videos (subscriber, view and video counts).

We do not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube users or viewers.

How we use and store data

  • Data is used only to upload our videos and to show our own channel statistics inside the tool.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising or profiling.
  • Stored channel statistics are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. On revocation we delete the related tokens and stored data within 7 days.
